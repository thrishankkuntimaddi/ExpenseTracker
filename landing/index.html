Know where your money goes.

Expenses, bills, budgets, savings and trips with friends — in one calm app that tells you what’s safe to spend today. On the web, Android, Mac, Windows and Linux.

What it does

Quick to log

Type “chai 20” or “petrol 1500” — it fills in the category and learns your choices.

A budget that paces you

What’s left this month, what’s safe to spend today, and where you’ll end up.

Bills that remind you

Rent, EMIs, SIPs and salary on schedule, with a reminder on the day.

Trips with friends

Everyone adds what they paid; it shows the fewest payments to settle up.

Savings goals

Track what you’re saving for and where the money is kept.

Private

App lock on every device and encrypted backups in your own Google Drive.

The same app on your phone and laptop

Add an expense on your phone and it’s on your laptop a moment later. Works offline, and every version updates itself.

A quick look

Logging an expense, checking the month, and the stats — in about 30 seconds.

Download
