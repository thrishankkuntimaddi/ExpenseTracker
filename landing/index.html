Private by design

Your data. Your Drive. Your lock.

🔒 App lock on every device

One PIN everywhere, fingerprint or face where available, and remote lock or sign-out from your phone.

☁️ Encrypted backups — in your Drive

Hidden in Google Drive’s app storage, sealed with AES-256, only the latest copy kept. Useless to anyone without your account.

🛡️ Alerts that matter, once

A new device signs in or someone tries wrong PINs? You hear about it once — on your devices and by email.

📤 Leave any time

Export everything to JSON or CSV whenever you like. No ads, no selling data, no lock-in.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes — no ads, no premium tier, no selling data. It’s open source under the MIT licence.

Where is my data kept?

In your own account on Google’s Firebase, readable only by you (shared trips: by the people on that trip). Backups go to your own Google Drive, encrypted. You can export everything any time.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Add and edit offline on any device; it syncs when you’re back online.

What about iPhone?

Open the web app in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It runs like an app, offline included.

Can my friends use it for a trip without installing anything?

Yes — they open your invite link in any browser, sign in or create a free account, and pick their name.
